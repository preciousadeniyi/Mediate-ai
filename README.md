# Mediate.ai

**Active inference–based team analytics for AI safety.**

Mediate.ai helps smart teams avoid bad decisions by measuring hidden group dynamics — power imbalances, silent groupthink, fragmented understanding, and unhealthy disagreement — using mathematically grounded active inference models.

## Why this exists

I kept watching smart, well-intentioned teams make catastrophic decisions. The problem wasn't intelligence or effort. It was invisible: one person dominating, quiet voices ignored, or everyone assuming they agreed when they didn't.

I taught myself active inference from scratch (no formal training — just papers, failure, and persistence) because I needed a framework that could quantify what teams can't see.

## What Mediate.ai measures

| Metric | What it tracks |
|--------|----------------|
| **Power Equity** | Balanced influence. Prevents dominance and surfaces quiet voices. |
| **Creative Tension** | Healthy cognitive diversity. Flags passive groupthink or chaotic friction. |
| **Narrative Coherence** | Shared storyline across the team. Detects fragmented understanding. |
| **Epistemic Convergence** | Structural agreement on facts. Quantifies real learning and alignment. |
